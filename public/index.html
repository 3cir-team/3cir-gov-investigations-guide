Certificate IV in Government Investigations (PSP40416) Through RPL

A plain-English guide for people who already investigate matters for government agencies and want that work recognised.

About the qualification

The Certificate IV in Government Investigations (PSP40416) is a nationally recognised AQF level 4 qualification from the Public Sector training package. It is also called the Cert IV or Cert 4 in Government Investigations. Through recognition of prior learning (RPL), 3CIR assesses the skills you already use, and Asset College issues the qualification.

What the units cover

PSP40416 has 15 units, made up of 6 core and 9 electives. The core units cover upholding public service values and principles, planning and initiating an investigation, conducting an investigation, finalising an investigation, applying regulatory powers, and gathering information through interviews.

Who can apply

Australian citizens and residents living in Australia can apply. No entry requirements are listed for PSP40416 on training.gov.au. Evidence such as investigation plans, interview records and reports can show you already use the skills in each unit, whether the work was paid, self-employed or voluntary.

A related qualification

If your work is in security vetting rather than investigations, see the Certificate IV in Government Security (Personnel Vetting) (PSP40316). It has 14 units, made up of 3 core and 11 electives, with a Personnel Vetting specialisation that needs at least 6 units from Group B. No entry requirements are listed for it either.
